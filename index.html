Safe & Simple Campus Process

How the UniFind Portal Works

Three straightforward steps designed to reconnect students and staff with misplaced property quickly and securely.

Step 1
📝

Report the Item

Submit a lost or found report with exact campus building, date, and distinguishing details.

Step 2
🔎

Filter & Connect

Browse campus listings, filter by zone or category, and communicate directly with finders or desk staff.

Step 3
🤝

Verify & Reunited

Verify ownership via security questions, collect at campus safe points, and mark the item reunited!
